Talk mode in In Situ, my Danish practice app, had one habit I couldn’t live with. Halfway through a sentence I’d reach for a word, ask “how do I say that?”, and my Danish partner would happily reply to it as if it were small talk. “Oh yes, yes, okay…” Lovely, but no help.

So I described it to Claude Code, from my phone, at 19:11.

The cause was small. The app heard a request, decided “that” was too vague to look up, and handed the turn back to the conversation.

Now “how do I say that?” (or “hvordan siger jeg det?”) makes the partner stop and wait. Whatever I say next, in English, comes back as a word card. There’s also a How do I say…? button for when the mic misses the question. It cuts the partner off mid-sentence, keeps what I’d already said so the lookup has context, and waits for the word.

Eleven minutes from noticing the bug to using the fix. That’s the part I keep thinking about. The people best placed to build software are the ones tripping over the problem, and it’s never been cheaper for them to just fix it.
